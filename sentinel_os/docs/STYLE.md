# Writing guide for engineering documents

> **Disclosure:** Parts of this guide came from a writing prompt that was previously in this repository. That prompt also asked that AI use be hidden. That part is dropped. Documents here may be written with AI help, and that should be stated where it matters.

1. **Start with the pressure.** Open with the problem, failure mode or constraint that forced the design, not with a description of the system.
2. **Make constraints and rejected alternatives visible.** For each major decision, say what it was chosen over and why that was rejected.
3. **State tradeoffs.** Say what was gained and what was given up.
4. **Say what is unknown.** Do not invent history, failures or results. Mark unverified claims as unverified.
5. **Use concrete verbs.** "Validates," "rejects," "isolates" say more than "optimizes," "enables," "orchestrates."
6. **Claim only what the evidence supports.** Prefer "reduces" to "eliminates" unless a test shows it.
7. **State each point once.** Avoid the definition, expanded definition, summary pattern.
8. **Cut filler.** Remove "furthermore," "moreover," "at its core," "it is important to note," and "in today's landscape."
